## Top 3 lessons

1. **The `SHONOKIN_ROOT` typo is still live, and it now sits next to the correct spelling.**
   [`README.md`](https://github.com/VeigaPunk/kimi-shokunin/blob/main/README.md) line 80 and
   [`MISSION.md`](https://github.com/VeigaPunk/kimi-shokunin/blob/main/skills/shokunin/protocol/MISSION.md)
   line 11 say `$SHONOKIN_ROOT`. But `MISSION.md` lines 22, 23 and 95 and
   [`shokunin-watchdog.sh`](https://github.com/VeigaPunk/kimi-shokunin/blob/main/scripts/shokunin-watchdog.sh)
   (lines 8–45) use `SHOKUNIN_ROOT`. Inside one file, the inconsistency is the clearest signal that
   it is a typo, not a deliberate alias. An operator who copies line 11 gets an empty variable, and
   the watchdog will fall back to its own parent directory without complaining.

2. **`SCORECARD.md` is now the hub of the craft round, but no seed or template exists for it.**
   It is named as the place to declare and record claims in
   [`commands/shokunin.md`](https://github.com/VeigaPunk/kimi-shokunin/blob/main/commands/shokunin.md),
   [`SKILL.md`](https://github.com/VeigaPunk/kimi-shokunin/blob/main/skills/shokunin/SKILL.md),
   [`AXES.md`](https://github.com/VeigaPunk/kimi-shokunin/blob/main/skills/shokunin/protocol/AXES.md),
   [`LEARNING.md`](https://github.com/VeigaPunk/kimi-shokunin/blob/main/skills/shokunin/protocol/LEARNING.md)
   (the expansion trend counts "a capability admitted to `SCORECARD.md`"),
   [`PROTOCOL.md`](https://github.com/VeigaPunk/kimi-shokunin/blob/main/skills/shokunin/protocol/PROTOCOL.md)
   and [`templates/round-prompt.md`](https://github.com/VeigaPunk/kimi-shokunin/blob/main/templates/round-prompt.md).
   No file named `SCORECARD*` exists anywhere in the tree, and `templates/mint/` does not seed one.
   Lanes are told to "create it per `AXES.md` if missing", so every lane builds its own first version,
   including the capabilities table that e737d2e added. That is a likely source of drift between lanes.
   Calibration on my Oct 19 note: `SCORECARD.md` is a real lane file, so listing it among mint outputs
   (branch `24f3982`) is wrong about *where it comes from*, not about whether it exists.

3. **A quiet window is a good time to look at review debt.** e737d2e (32 files, +1897/−289) is
   authored `ci <ci@shokunin.local>`, and LEARNING.md now requires a non-author spot-check of at
   least one admit and one reject per round. Nothing in the repo yet records an outside review of
   e737d2e itself. Separately, the `sources_gate` fail-open path (skip-with-warning when the census
   script is missing or returns an unexpected code) is unchanged. Both are worth settling before the
   next protocol amendment piles on.
