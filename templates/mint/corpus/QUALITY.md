## Source curation and mistake learning

Open a candidate before adding it to `SOURCES.md`; keep rejected sources with a reason
and a flip condition. Do not treat unopened leads or row count as learning. Each round
lesson records its admission gate and mines a mistake under `## Mistakes` as class,
evidence, captured-by and fix (or pastes the sweep that found none). A fix is evidence
only after the same check fails for the named reason in a red-before state; new quality
criteria cite the lesson that exposed the gap.
